      How Framewise works
      1. Paste a public YouTube video link, or paste a transcript directly.
      2. Framewise retrieves captions and creates a summary, key takeaways, learnings, and visual maps.
      3. Save notes in your browser library or export them as text or an SVG poster.
      Notes and your library are stored in this browser. API credentials stay on the server.
